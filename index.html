About

From debugging live networks to building the infrastructure behind them

I started my career testing the systems that carry everyone's phone calls and data — 5G, LTE, and VOLTE networks — tracing faults down to root cause and validating fixes before they reached real users. That work taught me to think in terms of what actually breaks in production, not just what works in a demo.

I'm now completing a Master's degree at the University of Oslo, redirecting that systems instinct toward cloud infrastructure: AWS, DevOps practices, and automation. This semester I'm taking IN5140, IN5040, and IN4230. In between, I've kept building — from a computer-vision system running on Kubernetes to a from-scratch network protocol implementation in C.

I'm looking for a place to apply both halves of that background: the discipline of testing real systems, and the momentum of learning cloud infrastructure fast.
